# Market Space Top Cells: Outbound Allocation Recommendations

Based on our analysis of the Crunchbase ODM sample, we clustered the market into (Sector, Size Band, AI Readiness) cells. By applying the Tenacious AI-maturity heuristics and overlaying our current delivery bench capacity, we isolated the cells with the highest "oxygen" for outbound prospecting. 

The three cells ranked highest on the combined score (hiring velocity + bench-match score + funding readiness) are detailed below.

## 1. Apps & Software | 11-50 Employees | AI Readiness 1 & 2
**Profile:**
This cell consists of early-to-mid stage Software/App companies that are actively scaling their teams. They show solid signals of AI openness (AI Readiness 1 or 2).
*   **Average Hiring Velocity:** ~2.4 to 2.6 open roles/month growth
*   **Bench-Match Score:** 9.3 to 10.0 (Extremely high alignment with Tenacious' Python/Go engineering bench)
*   **Outbound Recommendation:** 40% of outbound volume. Pitch Segment 1 (Recently funded scale-up). Lead with how scaling engineering output via an offshore squad is faster than in-house hiring.

## 2. Health Care | 1-10 Employees | AI Readiness 2
**Profile:**
Early-stage Health Care startups. These companies are small but highly motivated to implement AI tooling (AI Readiness 2), likely involving patient data parsing or compliance software.
*   **Average Hiring Velocity:** ~2.3 open roles/month growth
*   **Bench-Match Score:** 10.0 (Perfect alignment for data engineering needs)
*   **Outbound Recommendation:** 35% of outbound volume. Pitch Segment 4 (Specialized capability gaps). Focus on data platform engineering and ML integration where their small team lacks specialized in-house skills.

## 3. Banking & Finance | 101-250 Employees | AI Readiness 1
**Profile:**
Mid-market banking and financial services platforms. These are established companies with very high hiring velocity but lower foundational AI readiness.
*   **Average Hiring Velocity:** ~3.1 open roles/month growth
*   **Bench-Match Score:** 10.0 (High alignment for core infrastructure / data pipeline work)
*   **Outbound Recommendation:** 25% of outbound volume. Pitch Segment 2 (Mid-market restructuring/cost control). Do not pitch deep AI capability. Instead, focus on using offshore teams to manage massive infrastructure needs while controlling burn rate.
